Privacy Policy for Seven

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Seven (package name com.jakubguttner.seven) is a simple workout timer app. This page explains what data the app handles and why. Seven does not require an account, a sign-in, or a profile of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Seven keeps the following data only on your own device, for the app's own use:

  • Your workout history (dates and any exercise results you've recorded) and current streak
  • Your app preferences (timing, sounds, voice, exercise images, reminder settings)

None of this data is sent to us or to any third party. It exists only so the app can show you your own history and remember your preferences.

Backup to your Google Account

If your device is set up to back up apps to Google, Android's built-in Auto Backup feature may save the data above to a private storage area inside your own Google account, so it can be restored automatically if you reinstall the app or move to a new device signed into the same account. This is a standard Android OS feature, controlled by your device's own backup settings — we do not operate this storage and cannot access, read, or share what's backed up.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder or streak-protection notifications in Settings, they are scheduled entirely on your device. No data about these notifications is sent anywhere.

Anonymised usage statistics

We collect anonymised, aggregated statistics about how the app is used — such as which features are opened, how often workouts are completed, or app crash reports — to help us understand how Seven is used and to fix problems. This data is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identity, and is never sold or used for advertising.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your workout history, streak, or settings with anyone — that data stays on your device (and, if enabled, in your own Google account's backup storage, which we cannot access). Only the anonymised statistics described above are collected by us, and they cannot identify you.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Seven deletes all of its data from your device. If Google Auto Backup had saved a copy to your Google account, you can remove it from your device's backup settings (under your account's Backup settings) or it will be replaced automatically the next time you back up after uninstalling. Because anonymised statistics are never linked to your identity, there is no personal record tied to you to look up or delete; if you have questions, contact us below.

Children's privacy

Seven is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
