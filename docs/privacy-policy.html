Privacy Policy for Heartstreak: 7 Minute Workout

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Heartstreak: 7 Minute Workout (package name com.jakubguttner.seven) is a simple workout timer app. This page explains what data the app handles and why. Heartstreak does not require an account, a sign-in, or a profile of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Heartstreak keeps the following data only on your own device, for the app's own use:

  • Your workout history (dates and any exercise results you've recorded) and current streak
  • Your app preferences (timing, sounds, voice, exercise images, reminder settings)

None of this data is sent to us or to any third party. It exists only so the app can show you your own history and remember your preferences.

Backup to your Google Account

If your device is set up to back up apps to Google, Android's built-in Auto Backup feature may save the data above to a private storage area inside your own Google account, so it can be restored automatically if you reinstall the app or move to a new device signed into the same account. This is a standard Android OS feature, controlled by your device's own backup settings — we do not operate this storage and cannot access, read, or share what's backed up.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder or streak-protection notifications in Settings, they are scheduled entirely on your device. No data about these notifications is sent anywhere.

Health Connect

If you turn on the optional Health Connect integration in Settings, Heartstreak writes a record of each completed workout (as an exercise session) to Android's Health Connect, and, if you choose to log a breath count for a workout, a respiratory rate reading as well. This is stored by Health Connect on your own device under your control — you can review, manage, or revoke Heartstreak's access to it at any time from the Health Connect app, and you can turn the integration off in Heartstreak's Settings. We do not receive a copy of this data ourselves; it is only shared with other apps you have separately granted access to it through Health Connect.

In-app purchases

Heartstreak offers an optional purchase of extra hearts, processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google shares only what's needed to confirm the purchase (such as an order number), which the app uses on your device to refill your hearts and doesn't send anywhere.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Apart from the optional Health Connect integration described above, we do not share your workout history, streak, or settings with anyone — that data stays on your device (and, if enabled, in your own Google account's backup storage, which we cannot access). We do not collect any analytics, crash reports or identifiers.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Heartstreak deletes all of its data from your device. If Google Auto Backup had saved a copy to your Google account, you can remove it from your device's backup settings (under your account's Backup settings) or it will be replaced automatically the next time you back up after uninstalling. Workouts saved to Health Connect can be deleted in the Health Connect app. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete; if you have questions, contact us below.

Children's privacy

Heartstreak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
